🌾 PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

Direct cash transfer of ₹6,000 per annum paid in three equal installments of ₹2,000 directly into farmer bank accounts via DBT.

✅ Guaranteed annual income support for all landholding farmers.

Income Support • Central Govt

☔ PM Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)

Comprehensive crop insurance coverage against non-preventable natural risks: drought, flood, pests, and cyclones.

✅ Nominal premium rate (1.5% for Rabi, 2% for Kharif crops).

Crop Insurance • Risk Protection

🚜 SMAM Tractor & Equipment Subsidy

Sub-Mission on Agricultural Mechanization offers 40% to 50% subsidy for tractors, rotavators, power tillers, and harvesters.

✅ Up to ₹2.5 Lakh subsidy for individual farmers.

Farm Machinery • Modernization

☀️ PM KUSUM Solar Pump Scheme

Subsidy of up to 60% for farmers to install standalone solar water pumps (3HP to 7.5HP) in grid-disconnected regions.

✅ Zero electricity bill for irrigation with clean daytime power.

Solar Energy • Grid Independence

💧 PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)

Promotes Per Drop More Crop precision irrigation with up to 55% subsidy for micro-irrigation (drip and sprinkler systems).

✅ Saves 40-50% water while increasing yield by up to 30%.

Water Management • Drip Subsidy

🌱 Paramparagat Krishi Vikas (PKVY)

Cluster-based organic farming initiative providing ₹50,000 per hectare over 3 years for bio-fertilizers and organic certification.

✅ Free PGS organic certification and premium market linkage.

Organic Certification • Zero Budget
