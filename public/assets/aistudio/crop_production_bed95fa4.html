Rice Farming - Cultivation & Field Management

  • Land Preparation: Deep ploughing followed by 2-3 puddlings and leveling to sustain uniform standing water.
  • Soil Type: Alluvial clayey soils with pH 5.5 to 6.5 and high moisture holding capacity.
  • Seedbed & Nursery: 500 sq. meter nursery for 1 hectare field; sow treated seeds @ 25 kg/ha.
  • Transplanting: 20-25 day seedlings at 20x15 cm spacing with 2-3 seedlings per hill.
  • Water Schedule: Maintain 3-5 cm shallow standing water during tillering to panicle initiation.
  • Nutrient Management: 120:60:40 NPK kg/ha; apply Nitrogen in 3 splits (basal, tillering, panicle).
  • Expected Yield: 50-65 quintals/ha for improved hybrids; 35-45 quintals for Basmati varieties.
  • Top Varieties: IR-64, BPT-5204 (Samba Mahsuri), Pusa Basmati 1121, MTU-1010.

Wheat Farming - High Yield Protocols

  • Optimal Sowing Window: 25th October to 15th November in North-Western plains.
  • Seed Rate & Spacing: 100-120 kg/ha using seed drills at 20-22 cm row distance.
  • Critical Irrigation Stages: Crown root initiation (21 days), tillering (45 days), flowering (85 days), and milk stage.
  • Fertilizer Regimen: 120:60:40 NPK kg/ha + 25 kg Zinc Sulfate/ha.
  • Disease Control: For yellow rust, spray Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 ml/L at first appearance.
  • Expected Yield: 50-60 quintals/ha under irrigated conditions.
  • Top Varieties: HD-2967, HD-3086, PBW-550, DBW-187 (Karan Vandana), Sharbati.

Sugarcane Farming - Commercial Cash Crop

  • Planting Season: Autumn (Oct) or Spring (Feb-March); trench method with 90-120 cm row width.
  • Sett Treatment: Dip 2-budded setts in carbendazim (1g/L) for 15 minutes before furrow placement.
  • Fertilizer: 250:115:115 NPK kg/ha; split nitrogen over 3 months with 25 tons compost.
  • Ratoon Management: Stubble shaving, inter-row cultivation, and trash mulching saves 30% nitrogen.
  • Expected Yield: 80-110 tons/hectare with sugar recovery between 10.5% and 12.2%.
  • Top Varieties: Co-0238, Co-86032 (Nayana), Co-6304, Co-94012.

Maize Farming - Feed & Grain Management

  • Seasons: Kharif (June-July), Rabi (October-November), and Spring (January-February).
  • Spacing: 60 cm between rows and 20 cm between plants with 18-20 kg/ha hybrid seed.
  • Pest Management: Fall Armyworm (FAW) monitoring with pheromone traps; apply Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L.
  • Fertilization: 120:60:40 NPK kg/ha; apply 1/3 N + full P&K at planting; rest N at knee-high and tasseling.
  • Expected Yield: 60-75 quintals/ha with high starch hybrids.

Cotton Farming - Fiber & Textile Grade

  • Soil & Climate: Deep black cotton soil (Vertisols) with 600-1000 mm seasonal rainfall.
  • Spacing: 90x60 cm or 120x45 cm depending on canopy vigor and Bt hybrid type.
  • Integrated Pest Management (IPM): Grow castor or marigold border crops; install yellow sticky traps.
  • Irrigation: Critical at squaring, flowering, and boll development stages.
  • Expected Yield: 22-30 quintals/ha of seed cotton (Kapas).

High-Value Vegetable Cultivation

  • Tomatoes: Raised beds with drip irrigation and silver-black plastic mulch; stake indeterminate hybrids.
  • Chili & Capsicum: Nursery raised seedlings; treat against leaf curl thrips with Neem oil 10,000 ppm.
  • Onions: Rabi crop sown in Nov, transplanted Jan; yields 250-350 quintals/ha with 4-month storability.
  • Economics: Generates ₹3-5 Lakhs/ha gross revenue with multi-cropping and staggered harvesting.
